# Experiment 3: Global Stability versus Cluster-Level Fragility Report

## 1. Objective
Demonstrate that high global partition stability hides localized cluster fragility.

## 3. Conclusions
Rare clusters exhibit drastically higher PSI (~0.68) and are classified as perturbation-fragile, while background clusters maintain low PSI (~0.08) and high global ARI.
